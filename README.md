### Home Page:
This page consists of a slideshow pictures in the Background.
Then a cards consisting of best key features in AndhraPradesh like Spiritual Hub,Beaches,Rich culture etc.
Then it shows the top destinations where most of the people go even including foreiners.
In the end with a subsciption section which is designed to get a subscribtion just like a Youtube subscription where the subscribesrs get notifications when 
a new package or destination is updated.
Here in this page in the slide of image there exists a "discover now" button it directly lead you to "destinations" page. 
In the navigation bar section there exists a book now button when you click this it lead you to "Packages" page where you can book your desired package.


### Destinations :
This page shows all the major destinations available in AndhraPradest 
For example consider Tirupati:

Here you can see all the famous destionations and their small description about place, best time to visit,nearest airports and cities.
The discriptions will be visible like a flashcard popup when you click on the images of the destionations.
For this we used javaScript to make it possible and CSS to define its dimensions and styling.


### Packages :
Here there will be offers for all the packages like where it covers almost all the major and minor attractions along with the accomodation and food for the tourists.
It includes packages for number of members too.
This packges section gives you an idea of cost for a single person and when you click on the book option given in the image-card you will see a form which will automatically calculate the price according to the number of persons,children,number of days and nights.
For an adult its full price and for children below 13 years the price is half the price and for a day it's 60% and for night it's 40% of the defined amount.
After you enter your details along with one name and your telephone number,if you click on book now,You will see a success popup on top of the form showing your trip is booked successfully.
